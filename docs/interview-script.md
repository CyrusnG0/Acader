# Instructor interview script (OL-013)

Target mix (D-019): 3 Hong Kong university CS/engineering instructors or FYP supervisors + 2 remote instructors for comparison.

Goal: learn the real workaround, the buyer, and willingness to pay. **Interest is not demand.** Do not pitch. Take verbatim notes. Source decisions: D-011, D-012, D-017, D-018.

## Opening (one question, ask first, then be quiet)
1. "What did you do last semester when you suspected a student's code (or essay) was AI-written?"

## Workaround and cost
2. How many hours did that take, and who did it (you, TAs, an integrity office)?
3. What do you do for oral checks or in-class quizzes today? What breaks?
4. What would you do differently if it cost you nothing?

## Willingness to pay (D-018: three hypotheses, never state a price first)
5. Does your department pay for any tool in this area today (Turnitin, Codio, Gradescope, anything)? What and roughly how much?
6. Who would approve a purchase: you, the programme director, the department head, central IT? How long did the last tool take to buy?
7. Which feels most realistic to you: (H1) a licence you buy for your own course, (H2) a small per-student fee added to existing tools, (H3) a free trial on one assignment, then a department decision? Why?
8. What would it have to replace or save for you to pay for it?

## Do-it-yourself check (D-017)
9. Have you seen NYU Stern's free Viva tool or tried anything similar? If a colleague built a voice viva for $0.42 per student, would you just use that? What would make you not?

**Optional 10-minute side-by-side (D-020):** with the instructor's consent, run NYU Viva (viva.stern.nyu.edu) and the Room demo/runner on the same small task. Ask: "Which would you use for your course, and why?" Note whether the reason is grounding in your course, trust/defensibility, student ownership, price, or "no difference". Never demo with real student data (the demo sends text to DeepSeek).

## Close (ask for behaviour, not opinion)
10. Would you run one real assignment through a practice viva with a handful of students next month? (Consent-first, ungraded: see D-014.)
11. Who else should I talk to?

## After each interview
Write one paragraph: what they actually do, who pays, any price number they said, anything surprising. Update `docs/OPEN_LOOPS.md` OL-013/OL-014.
