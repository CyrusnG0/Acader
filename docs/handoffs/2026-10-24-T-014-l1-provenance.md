## What changed
All new code is in `prototype/server/engine/provenance/`. Everything is pure, uses integers only, and `canonical_json` is enforced on the output.

- **`sentences.py`: `split_sentences(text) -> [(start, end)]`**, applied per textblock, so a sentence never spans paragraphs.
  - A hard break always ends a sentence.
  - A terminator is a run of `.!?…` plus any closing quotes or brackets. It ends a sentence only when whitespace or the end of the text follows. That is how "3.5", "e.g.,", "U.S.A" and URLs stay inside one sentence.
  - Even then, there is no break in these cases:
    - the next letter (after any opening quote or bracket) is lowercase;
    - the token is a known abbreviation (`e.g.`, `i.e.`, `cf.`, `vs.`, `al.` (as in "et al."), `fig.`, `p.`, `pp.`, `dr.`, …);
    - the token is an initial or initialism (`J.`, `U.S.`, `a.m.`). A lone initial right after a lowercase word does end the sentence ("found X. Next" splits);
    - the token is a leading list number ("1. First").
  - `etc.` ends a sentence when the next word is capitalised.
- **`trail.py`:** rebuilds cards, anchors and claims (student or declared) from events.
  - A card's chain ends in `block`, `coach` or `import`, or it breaks with `card_not_found`, `card_pasted` (≥ 50% of the card's characters not covered by typed runs) or `card_not_anchored`.
  - A declared claim counts as re-anchored once one of its live cards ends in a block.
  - `card.edit` never revives a deleted card, and keeps the card's `via` when the payload leaves it out.
- **`bands.py`:** the core of the task.
  - Walks every textblock (`p1…`) with each character's ProseMirror position (UTF-16) and origin.
  - Applies the T-013 mark-check results. A checked kind that failed, or that no check result covers, counts as `unconfirmed`.
  - Produces the per-sentence origin mix (8 keys), the chains through paragraph links, the bands, the notes, the claim units, the integer proportions and the L3b queue.
- **`facts.py`:** the provenance §7 facts:
  - reading depth per source: block kinds opened and anchored;
  - thinking moments (a)–(d), each with its seq and ts;
  - the Coach record by case, pillar and latest outcome;
  - paste facts (editor pastes with their surviving characters, cards, Coach answers);
  - replacement edits, marked `lower_bound: true`;
  - declared help by tool, and how many declared claims were later anchored;
  - unanchored claims.

  It computes no construction-order measure and no grading of Coach answers (D-046).
- **`l1.py`:**
  - `L1Config` holds every threshold: `paste_ext_pct=50`, `declared_import_pct=50`, `unlinked_untyped_pct=30`, `min_quote_words=4`, and the flag `l3b=False`. `to_json()` returns `{"flags", "thresholds"}` for the engine stamp, which T-016 consumes.
  - `compute_l1(doc, runs, events, seal_seq=…, l3b=(), config=…)` drops events after the seal, so post-seal `link.add` never changes bands (CEO-17).
  - `l1_from_compile(CompileResult, events)` runs it on a T-013 compile result.
- **`__init__.py`:** adds the new exports.
- **Tests (3 files, 72 tests):**
  - `server/tests/test_l1_sentences.py`
  - `server/tests/test_l1_bands.py` (includes one end-to-end test: `EventService` → `compile_marks` → `l1_from_compile`)
  - `server/tests/test_l1_facts.py`

### Band rules as built (first match wins; provenance §6)
Each sentence counts its characters by kind: imp, quo and coa are confirmed import, quote and coach characters; pas is `paste_ext`; unc is unconfirmed; n is the total. The sentence is `clean` when pas + unc = 0.

1. **declared:** imp × 100 ≥ 50 × n, or the paragraph links a declared claim that was never re-anchored.
2. **traced_source:** no `added` propositions, `clean`, and either a confirmed quote character, or (only with L3b on) an L3b confirmation for a link without a `suggestion_id` whose chain ends in a block.
3. **traced_coach:** the same with a confirmed coach character, or an L3b chain ending in `coach`.
4. **student_linked:** the paragraph has any link, whatever its `by`, and (pas + unc) × 100 < 50 × n. This applies D13, and an unconfirmed label counts like a paste.
5. **untraced:** everything else. Notes: `pasted_linked_not_checked` → "pasted; linked by the student, not checked"; `label_not_confirmed` → "this label couldn't be confirmed against its source".

**Claim units:** each linked claim, plus each non-empty unlinked paragraph. A declared claim that was never re-anchored is `declared`; otherwise a unit takes the band with the most words, and a tie goes to the later band.

**Output counts:**
- `bands`: `total_words`, `by_words`, `by_claims`, `quoted_words`, `inferred_links` (paragraph links that carry a `suggestion_id`), `map_suggestions`.
- L3b results passed while `l3b` is off raise `ValueError`.

  *Recommend:* add both to event-log §3 with the Door and Coach tasks.

- **PD-T014-3: when a quote makes a sentence traced (band 2 at L1).** As built: any confirmed quote character, as long as the sentence has no `paste_ext` and no unconfirmed characters. The typed framing around a checked quote is traced with it. *Alternative:* require quote characters to be the majority of the sentence. *Recommend:* keep, and revisit on the golden sessions.
- **PD-T014-4: an unconfirmed label counts like a paste for D13** (fail closed). A forged mark in a linked paragraph can't reach band 4. *Recommend:* accept, and add it to provenance §6.
- **PD-T014-5: a link from a map suggestion can never be traced, even when L3b confirms it** (D19 read strictly). As written, D27 would allow it. *Recommend:* accept (strict).
- **PD-T014-6: `added > 0` also blocks band 3** (Coach). Provenance §6 says this only for band 2. *Recommend:* accept, for symmetry.
- **PD-T014-7: claim units.**
  - Units are the claims linked in the document plus every non-empty unlinked paragraph, so `by_claims` isn't empty for an essay with no links.
  - A unit's band is its majority band by words; a tie goes to the later (more cautious) band.
  - A declared claim that was never re-anchored is `declared`.

  *Recommend:* accept, and write it in provenance §6.
- **PD-T014-8: headings** have no links and are banded and counted like any sentence, so a heading is usually untraced (a few words). *Alternative:* leave headings out of the word counts. *Recommend:* leave them out, in a follow-up, if the golden sessions show visible noise.
- **PD-T014-9: sentences made only of Helper text** (a formatted reference list) get no special treatment, so they are untraced unless linked. That inflates untraced for students with a reference list. *Recommend:* a founder decision before T-016. Options: (a) keep; (b) exclude sentences that are entirely confirmed Helper text from the counts, as "mechanics".
- **PD-T014-10: the sentence-split rule** above, including "a lone initial after a lowercase word ends the sentence", which over-splits "said J. Smith". *Recommend:* accept as v1 and tune it on the golden set.
- **PD-T014-11: smaller choices.**
  - Anchors accumulate per card (a new anchor doesn't replace the old one).
  - A card is "pasted" when ≥ 50% of its characters are not covered by typed runs (the same threshold as D13).
  - Thinking moment (a) needs the anchor and the limitation pin before the claim edit; if several sources qualify, it reports the smallest source_id.
  - A `card.set_aside` with no `reason_tag` is not a moment, and the note text is never copied.

  *Recommend:* accept.
