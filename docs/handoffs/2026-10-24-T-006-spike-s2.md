## Result in one paragraph
**Docling** (OCR off) is the only parser that passes the S2 bar on all three readings. It found all 22 expected kinds and all 27 tables with their cell structure, 37 of 38 headings and about one block per reference. With OCR off, the scan comes back as "scan". **GROBID** (CRF image) fails the bar: it found 5 of 27 tables (the nursing tables came back as figures), and the scan returned HTTP 500. **PyMuPDF** passes on quality but is noisy: 108 extra "headings" across the 3 readings, the socsci abstract was missed, and references were split. It is also **AGPL-3.0**, which matters for a hosted service. Docling's costs: 1.6 GB of Python packages + ~1 GB of models, a **4.3 GB peak memory** on the 86-page reading, and 0.36 s per page on 2 CPU threads (≈ 110 s for 300 pages on this machine; a cloud vCPU may be slower [ASSUMPTION]). Full numbers are in RESULT.md.

## Proposed decisions (the checker takes these to the founder; nothing here is decided)
- **PD-1: Docling is the v1 parser** (OCR off, table structure on, in the jobs loop, models baked into the image, converter loaded once per worker). *Recommended: yes.* It is the only one of the three that passes S2 on all three readings, it is MIT (models CDLA-Permissive-2.0 / Apache-2.0), and it runs in process.
- **PD-2: a text-layer check runs before parsing.** pypdfium2 (already a Docling dependency): fewer than 50 characters per page → status "scan", with no model loaded. *Recommended: yes.* It takes under 0.1 s and does not depend on the parser.
- **PD-3: no PyMuPDF in product code** while it is AGPL-3.0, unless the founder accepts AGPL obligations for the hosted service or buys Artifex's commercial licence. *Recommended: don't use it.* Spike and test tooling outside the product are unaffected.
- **PD-4: host sizing for parsing.** One parse at a time and at least 8 GB RAM on the pilot machine. Before M2, measure Docling's peak memory with a smaller page batch and time a 300-page reading on the real host. If it misses the 300 s cap, parse in page ranges. *Recommended: yes*, since architecture.md §4 assumes one machine.
- **PD-5 (later, not v1): GROBID for reference metadata** in citation help (§6, structured authors/title/DOI). *Recommended:* add to TODOS; don't build now.
