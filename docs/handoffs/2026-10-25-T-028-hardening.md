## What changed

1. **Engine version bump.** `prototype/server/engine/receipt/compile.py`: `ENGINE_VERSION` is now
   `"receipt/1 l1/2"` (the `l1` part only). `RECEIPT_SCHEMA` stays `1`. The compiled body gained
   `more_places` in T-027 and `skipped_no_grounding` now counts ungrounded candidates across the
   whole candidate list, so the stamp moves.

   No golden fixture or pinned hash needed editing. The goldens freeze projections
   (`expected_receipt`, `expected_compiled_receipt`), not the engine stamp or the receipt hash;
   their hash tests compare the runtime receipt with its own canonical bytes. The full suite
   passes without regenerating anything, so the documented recording route was not run.

2. **Close test gap L1.** `prototype/server/tests/test_receipt_more_places.py`:
   - New fixture `two_candidate_log()`: the five-place log, but p1 (only in `more_places`) has two
     student claims (`k1`, `k1b`) and p2 (chosen) has two declared claims (`kd`, `kd4`). Each
     claim is grounded through its own claim text, and each pair anchors to the same paragraph.
   - `test_one_place_per_paragraph_never_repeats` now uses that log. It first proves the fixture
     really carries two claim units for p1 and two for p2, and checks p2 is chosen while p1 is in
     `more_places`. It then asserts `more_places` has at most one entry per `paragraph_id` and no
     `paragraph_id` that already produced a chosen question.
   - Helper `claim_units_for(l1, paragraph_id)` groups L1 claim units by paragraph; it is local to
     this test file and follows the surrounding style.
