URBP 3010 · Housing Policy Workshop

Week 9 writing task — one essay, untimed, done once. Submit from this page.

The question

"Should Hong Kong tighten rent control on subdivided units? Take a position and defend it with evidence from the course archive."

Requirements

  • 500–800 words, a clear position, and at least 6 linked citations.
  • A citation is a claim anchored to an archive passage you personally retrieved (see §Write). Citations to passages you never retrieved are rejected.
  • The Librarian answers with passages only. It will never summarise, conclude, or argue for you.

How this room works

  • Sealed archive — documents are listed by title, but contents only open through the Librarian (or one passage at a time, revealed after you commit a question).
  • Your questions are the process evidence. They are marked (FACT / DEEP / BROAD / CONCLUSION-SEEK) and shape your receipt.
  • The receipt is not a grade. It is a coherence profile of your process, exported with the essay.

Scratchpad

Outline, doubts, positions you considered and dropped. Exported with your receipt — thinking counts.

Pasting long text is recorded as a structure flag. It is not forbidden — it is just visible.

SESSION DATA — export for assessment · import to score any session

Scoring is mechanical and browser-side: traceability (citations ↔ your own retrievals), continuity (essay vocabulary ↔ what you asked for and received), orphan rate, structure. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
