# Five-minute demo script

**Say first (20 seconds):** "Students do graded work in this room. The AI helps them think but never writes for them. When the work is handed in, it shows where an idea has no earlier step in the room, as a question for a person to ask. It is not a verdict."

1. **Show the result first (60 seconds).** Open **How it compares**. Point at the honest student next to the outside-AI routes. "These are 84 simulated students. Honest work with visible thinking scores about 88. Every outside route had a median between 34 and 55. The one that reads our rules and loops on our questions had to type as much as an honest student." Then point at the card for the honest student who barely talks: "This is the limit. Silent thinking leaves no trace, so a person is asked to hear it. That is why the live conversation is the real gate."
2. **Go to the Room and work like a student (2 minutes).**
   - Click a starter: *Find me a source on how rents moved in subdivided units*. The assistant does not hand over a list. It asks what the source has to show.
   - Answer in a sentence. A shuffled shortlist appears. Pick two sources and give a reason.
   - Open a passage and read it.
   - Type *write my intro for me*. It refuses and asks for your idea instead.
   - Use **State my own claim** with one claim and why. The Coach replies with one question.
3. **Hand in (60 seconds).** Open the **Draft** tab, write four or five sentences citing the passage you read, like `[D6p1]`, and press **Submit and see the report**. Show the three counts (built in the room, explained after, no earlier trace) and the questions.
4. **The contrast (optional).** Start a **New session**, ask for nothing, paste in a finished essay with citations and submit. The report is a list of questions about ideas that have no earlier step.

**If asked about limits, say plainly:** all results so far come from simulated students, not real people. A real pilot with a live oral is the next step, and a draft plan exists (`PILOT-PROTOCOL.md`).

**Do not say:** that it prevents anything, or that it proves understanding. It produces evidence and questions.
