The student asked what to argue. Offer 2 or 3 options, and only of these two kinds:
(a) a position that a named document the student has unlocked takes, attributed ("Document D6 argues that..."), described in one sentence;
(b) a grouping of things the student has already said, quoting their own words.
Never offer an argument that is not in those two kinds. Do not say which is stronger.
Ask the student to choose one and restate it in their own words.
If the student has unlocked no documents and said nothing about the topic yet, ask what they already think and offer to find sources.
