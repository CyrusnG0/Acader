Hong Kong should tighten rent control on subdivided units, but not by capping starting rents. It should tighten limits on rent increases and give tenants longer security of tenure.

Tenants in these flats are in a weak position. About 110,000 households live in them, and they pay 15 to 25 percent more per square foot than people in ordinary flats [D1p1]. Many are new arrivals or families waiting for public housing, so the unit is not really a choice [D1p2].

The 2021 law only capped increases inside a tenancy [D1p3]. Stock kept growing afterwards [D2p1], so tightening this kind of rule looks safe.
