I think Hong Kong should tighten rules on subdivided units, but only a little. About 110,000 households live in these units, and many are waiting for public housing for around five and a half years [D1p2]. They pay more per square foot than people in ordinary flats [D1p1].

The 2021 law capped rent increases inside a tenancy but not starting rents [D1p3]. The number of units kept growing afterwards [D2p1]. So a mild rule did not seem to make landlords leave.

A hard cap is different. The working paper says caps on rent levels shrink supply [D6p2]. Berlin shows the same thing, where listings fell after the cap [D7p1].
