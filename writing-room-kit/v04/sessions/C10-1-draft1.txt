I think Hong Kong should tighten the rules on subdivided units, but not by lowering rents with a hard cap. The better change is to make tenants safe from eviction and to limit rent increases within a tenancy.

The 2021 ordinance limited increases within tenancies and the stock of units kept growing from 100,800 to 111,200 [D2p1]. So mild control did not make landlords leave. But rent burden still rose from 31 to 34 percent [D2p2], so the current rule is not enough.

A hard cap is risky. Hard caps on rent levels shrink supply, and the people who lose units are the poor [D6p1][D6p3].

Enforcement is the weak point. Inspections cover under 6 percent of stock [D10p1], and tenants with no written agreement cannot complain [D10p2]. Mr Chan was given two weeks notice after he asked for a repair [D3p1].
