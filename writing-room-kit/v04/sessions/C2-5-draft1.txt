I think Hong Kong should tighten rent control on subdivided units, but only in a moderate way. Rent control helps tenants and it does not seem to make landlords leave the market.

The data shows that the number of subdivided units kept growing. In 2019 there were about 100,800 units and by 2025 there were 111,200 [D2p1]. This growth continued after the 2021 tenancy control ordinance, so moderate regulation did not cause visible stock exit.

But rents are still a problem. Rents per square foot in these units rose faster than general private rents [D2p2].
