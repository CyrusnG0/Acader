I think Hong Kong should tighten rent control on subdivided units, but not with a hard cap. Landlords say they will leave if returns fall, but they admit they have no data on their real margins [D5p3]. A working paper says hard caps shrink the stock and hurt the poor tenants they are meant to help, while limits on increases within a tenancy have small effects [D6p1] [D6p2].
