**Tighten the Tenancy, Not the Rent: A Case Against a Hard Cap on Subdivided Units**

Hong Kong should not tighten rent control on subdivided units (SDUs) by capping initial rents. It should instead tighten tenure security and enforcement of living conditions, paired at most with a light, indexed band on first rents. The archive points consistently to one conclusion: a hard cap on initial rent levels would transfer income to today's incumbents while shrinking the residual stock on which tomorrow's most vulnerable households depend, and it would do so through an enforcement apparatus that cannot reach the tenants it claims to protect.
