Subdivided units in Hong Kong are flats carved into small cubicles, home to about 110,000 households [D1p1]. Tenants pay a premium per square foot even though the total rent is lower [D1p1].

My early view is that Hong Kong should tighten rent rules, but carefully. The stock kept growing after the 2021 ordinance [D2p1], so moderate regulation did not seem to make units disappear. Still, the rent burden got worse, from 31 to 34 percent of income [D2p2].

Mr Chan says insecurity and the housing waiting list matter more to him than the rent itself [D3p1, D3p3].
