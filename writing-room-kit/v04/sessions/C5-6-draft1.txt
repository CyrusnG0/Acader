Hong Kong should tighten protection for subdivided unit tenants, but it should do it through security of tenure and inspections, not a hard rent cap.

Tenants say rent level is not their main problem. In a survey of 440 households, 61 percent would accept a small rent increase for three years of secure tenure [D11p1]. The report says caps treat the price symptom of a queue problem [D11p2].

Moderate regulation has not made owners leave. Stock grew from 100,800 units in 2019 to 111,200 in 2025, including after the 2021 ordinance [D2p1].

A hard cap is risky. Berlin's cap on 1.5 million flats made listings shrink within a year [D7p1], and landlords say a cap is confiscatory given thin margins [D5p1].
