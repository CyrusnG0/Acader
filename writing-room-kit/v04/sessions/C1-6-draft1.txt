Subdivided units (SDUs) in Hong Kong house about 110,000 households, and they pay more per square foot than people in ordinary flats [D1p1]. Most are new arrivals, single parents and people waiting for public housing [D1p2].

My early view is that a harder rent cap on SDUs may not help the people it is meant to protect. A tenant, Mr Chan, would save about 800 dollars a month but fears landlords would shut cubicles instead [D3p2]. A working paper says hard caps on rent levels shrink supply [D6p2], and Berlin saw fewer listings after its cap [D7p1].
