Claim: Hong Kong should tighten rent control on subdivided units, but only with a cap on rent increases inside a tenancy, paired with tenure security and funded enforcement. It should not use a hard cap on initial rents.

Evidence: Stock of subdivided units kept growing after the 2021 ordinance [D2p1], and the working paper says increase caps have small or null effects on stock, while hard caps shrink it [D6p2]. A hard 15 percent cut in initial rents would help incumbents but block 3,000 to 4,000 households a year [D6p4].

Objection: The NGO says tenants want tenure security more than lower rent [D11p1].

Reply: The same report supports a light band on rents [D11p3], so it does not reject limits.
