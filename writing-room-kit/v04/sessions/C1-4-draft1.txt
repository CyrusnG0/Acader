Hong Kong should not adopt a hard cap on the starting rent of subdivided units. About 110,000 households live in them, and they pay 15 to 25 percent more per square foot than in ordinary flats [D1p1]. The rent burden got worse from 31 to 34 percent of income [D2p2]. This is a real problem, but a hard cap could make it worse for the people it is meant to help [D6p3].

The 2021 ordinance capped only increases within a tenancy [D1p3]. Stock kept growing after it [D2p1].
