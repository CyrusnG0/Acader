My view so far is that Hong Kong should tighten rules on subdivided units only in a narrow way. A hard cap on rents looks risky. One working paper estimates that a 10 percent cap-induced rent cut removes about 3 to 5 percent of stock over five years [D6p1]. Berlin's rent cap made new listings shrink within a year [D7p1].

But Hong Kong stock kept growing under the 2021 ordinance [D2p1], and that ordinance only limits increases inside a tenancy [D6p2]. Tenants in a survey said they would trade rent for three years of security [D11p1].
