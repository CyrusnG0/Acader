I got there by working through the evidence in the archive rather than starting with a position. The supply-elasticity and Berlin material [D6p1][D6p4][D7p1] showed a hard cap would shrink entry, but the tenants' testimony [D3p1][D11p1] and the enforcement data [D10p1] showed rent levels still matter and any cap needs to be administrable. The legal opinion [D9p1][D9p2] gave me the actual instrument—a published, indexed per-square-foot band—which fit both constraints, so I built the thesis around it.
