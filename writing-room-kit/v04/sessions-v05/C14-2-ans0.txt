I got there by working through the evidence rather than starting with a position: the stock data and elasticity estimates showed a hard cap would shrink supply, while the Tenants' stories and the NGO survey showed tenure security was what people actually wanted. So I combined the two—a light indexed band plus security of tenure—as the option the evidence pointed to. The legal opinion on Basic Law risk and the consultation's expected "floor-standard + rent band" package confirmed that this middle path was the realistic one.
